# [A mean–maximum inequality for independent sets of forests](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We prove that the mean size of an independent set of any finite forest is at least 0.47 times its independence number. Exact cone certificates establish the bound, and the minimum normalized position of a weak local peak converges to a constant between 0.47 and 0.473403.
