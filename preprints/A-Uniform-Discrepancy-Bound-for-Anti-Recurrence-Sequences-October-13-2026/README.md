# [A uniform discrepancy bound for anti-recurrence sequences](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
13 October 2026

For every positive coefficient vector with at least two entries, we prove a uniform bound |Aₙ − κn| ≤ 2κ for the associated anti-recurrence sequence. This establishes the boundedness form of the conjecture on these sequences.
