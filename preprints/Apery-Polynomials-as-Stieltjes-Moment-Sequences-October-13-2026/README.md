# [Apéry polynomials as Stieltjes moment sequences](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
13 October 2026

We prove that the Apéry polynomials form a Stieltjes moment sequence for every real x ≥ 1. We give an explicit compactly supported probability density that is positive in the interior of its support.
