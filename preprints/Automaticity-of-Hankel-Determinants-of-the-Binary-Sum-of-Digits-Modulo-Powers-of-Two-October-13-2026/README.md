# [Automaticity of Hankel determinants of the binary sum of digits modulo powers of two](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
13 October 2026

We prove that Hankel determinants of the binary sum-of-digits sequence, reduced modulo any power of two, form a 2-automatic sequence. A normalized recursion yields a finite automaton, while the unreduced determinant sequence is not 2-regular.
