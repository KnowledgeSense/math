# [Chollet’s permanent inequality for Hermitian matrices whose comparison matrices are positive semidefinite](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
8 October 2026

We prove Chollet’s permanent inequality in every order for Hermitian matrices whose comparison matrices are positive semidefinite. The result includes scaled diagonally dominant matrices and graph Laplacians with nonnegative edge weights.
