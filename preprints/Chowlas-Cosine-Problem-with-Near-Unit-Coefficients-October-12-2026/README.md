# [Chowla’s cosine problem with near-unit coefficients](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

For N distinct positive integer frequencies and real coefficients in [0.99, 1.01], we prove that the cosine sum has minimum at most −N^(1/9)/20. The proof adapts a Fourier-analytic construction and controls the errors caused by varying coefficients.
