# [Circular permutations of finite fields with primitive adjacent products](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
10 October 2026

We classify circular permutations of the nonzero elements of finite fields whose adjacent products become primitive after adding a fixed offset. For every prime power q > 7, the only exceptional pair is (q, a) = (11, 2).
