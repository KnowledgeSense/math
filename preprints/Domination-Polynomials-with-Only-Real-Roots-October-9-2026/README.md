# [Domination polynomials with only real roots](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
9 October 2026

We classify the finite simple graphs whose domination polynomials have only real roots, confirming Oboudi’s conjecture. They are obtained by attaching one or two pendant vertices to every vertex of a graph and adding isolated vertices.
