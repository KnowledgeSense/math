# [EFX allocations of chores that are cheap for exactly two agents](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
7 October 2026

We prove the existence of complete EFX allocations when every chore costs 1 to exactly two agents and a common r > 2 to the others, for any number of agents. Together with known results, this establishes existence for every r > 1.
