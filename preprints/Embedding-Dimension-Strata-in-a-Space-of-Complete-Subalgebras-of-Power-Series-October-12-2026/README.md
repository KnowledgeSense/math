# [Embedding dimension strata in a space of complete subalgebras of power series](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

For the value semigroup ⟨10, 11, 34, 46, 58⟩, we determine the space of complete power-series subalgebras and all its embedding dimension strata. The four-generator locus provides counterexamples to two proposed descriptions of these strata.
