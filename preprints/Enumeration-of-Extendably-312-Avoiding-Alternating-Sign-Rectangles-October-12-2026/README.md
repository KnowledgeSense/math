# [Enumeration of extendably 312-avoiding alternating sign rectangles](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We prove the conjectured three-parameter enumeration formula for extendably 312-avoiding alternating sign rectangles. Diagonal generating functions solve the recurrences and identify the proposed formula through Lagrange inversion.
