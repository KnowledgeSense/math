# [Gamma-positivity of h-polynomials of preorders](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
7 October 2026

We prove that the h-polynomial of every finite preorder is gamma-positive. Its gamma coefficients count disjoint pairs of equally sized subsets admitting an upward bijection.
