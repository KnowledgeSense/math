# [General line cones and the leading-form map](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We prove that general line cones in affine four-space have no negative-degree cotangent cohomology for a set of branch numbers of natural density one. These cones admit no algebraic or analytic smoothing. The proof combines leading-form maps, explicit finite certificates and induction.
