# [Hilbert coefficients of complete bipartite quadratic ideals](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We compute every Hilbert coefficient of the ideal generated by the edges of a complete bipartite graph and all variable squares. Their signs are classified by the smaller part size, yielding counterexamples to the nonnegativity conjecture for Artinian quadratic algebras.
