# [Local permutation polynomials of maximum degree with coefficients in the prime field](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
7 October 2026

We construct local permutation polynomials of total degree n(q−2) with coefficients in the prime field for every prime power q > 3 and every n ≥ 1. The proof uses Frobenius-orbit exchanges and an exact coefficient computation in characteristic three.
