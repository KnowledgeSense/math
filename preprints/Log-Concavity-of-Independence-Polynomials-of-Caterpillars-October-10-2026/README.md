# [Log-concavity of independence polynomials of caterpillars](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
10 October 2026

We prove that the independence polynomial of every finite caterpillar graph is log-concave. The result holds for arbitrary numbers of leaves attached to the spine vertices.
