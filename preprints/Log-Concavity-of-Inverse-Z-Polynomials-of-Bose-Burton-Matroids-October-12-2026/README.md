# [Log-concavity of inverse Z-polynomials of Bose–Burton matroids](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We prove strict log-concavity of inverse Z-polynomial coefficients for all Bose–Burton matroids, including affine geometries. After normalization by projective-geometry coefficients, the sequence is positive and concave.
