# [Log-concavity of inverse Z-polynomials of Dowling matroids with arbitrary joints](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We prove positivity and strict log-concavity of inverse Z-polynomial coefficients for Dowling matroids with any subset of joints, over every finite group. The polynomial depends only on the number of vertices, the group order and the number of retained joints.
