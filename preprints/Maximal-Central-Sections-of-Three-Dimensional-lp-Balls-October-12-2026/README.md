# [Maximal central sections of three-dimensional ℓp-balls](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

For every p > 2, we classify the maximal central planar sections of the three-dimensional ℓp-ball. The maximizing normals change from (1, 1, 1) to (1, 1, 0) at a unique exponent in (4.99, 5), with both families maximizing at the transition.
