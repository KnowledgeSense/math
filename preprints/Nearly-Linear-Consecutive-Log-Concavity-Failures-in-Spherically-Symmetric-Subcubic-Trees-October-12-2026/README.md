# [Nearly linear consecutive log-concavity failures in spherically symmetric subcubic trees](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We construct spherically symmetric trees of maximum degree three with nearly linearly many consecutive log-concavity failures in their independence sequences. The failures stay a fixed fractional distance below the independence number.
