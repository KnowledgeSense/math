# [Nonvanishing of the residues of the heat kernel for a logarithmic Laplacian on the circle](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
13 October 2026

We prove that every coefficient governing the candidate residues of the diagonal heat kernel for the circle’s logarithmic Laplacian is nonzero, and determine its exact 2-adic valuation. Consequently, all the predicted half-integer poles occur.
