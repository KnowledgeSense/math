# [Numerical semigroups of depth two with prescribed Buchweitz profiles](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We construct numerical semigroups of depth two with arbitrary prescribed finite Buchweitz profiles and negative values beyond the prescribed range. Consequently, every finite subset of {2, 3, …} occurs as a Buchweitz set.
