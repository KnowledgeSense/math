# [Optimal constants in the Ando–Hiai–Okubo trace inequality](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
9 October 2026

We determine the optimal constant in the Ando–Hiai–Okubo trace inequality for every alternating word and matrix size. In dimensions at least three the constant is one exactly when an adjacent-weight condition holds, and is infinite otherwise.
