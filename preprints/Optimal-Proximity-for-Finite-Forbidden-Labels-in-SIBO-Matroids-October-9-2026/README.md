# [Optimal proximity for finite forbidden labels in SIBO matroids](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
9 October 2026

We prove the sharp exchange bound ∏ₜ(|Fₜ|+1)−1 for reaching a feasible basis of a SIBO matroid with finitely many forbidden labels in abelian groups. For singleton forbidden sets the bound is 2ᵏ−1.
