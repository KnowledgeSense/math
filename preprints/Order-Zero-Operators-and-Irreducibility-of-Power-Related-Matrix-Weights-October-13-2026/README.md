# [Order-zero operators and irreducibility of power-related matrix weights](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
13 October 2026

We prove that power-related matrix weights in the stated family are irreducible in every size, for both proposed exponent lists. Their order-zero differential operators are precisely the scalar multiples of the identity.
