# [Polynomial counts of cokernel-closed subcategories with fixed first-column defect](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
13 October 2026

For representations of a linearly oriented type A quiver, we prove that the number of cokernel-closed additive subcategories with fixed first-column defect i is a polynomial of degree 2i with leading coefficient 1/i!. A finite-template expansion gives an explicit binomial-basis formula.
