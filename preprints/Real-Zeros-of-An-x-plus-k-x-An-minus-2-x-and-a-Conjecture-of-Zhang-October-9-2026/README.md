# [Real zeros of Aₙ(x)+kxAₙ₋₂(x) and a conjecture of Zhang](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
9 October 2026

We determine exactly which real k make Aₙ(x)+kxAₙ₋₂(x) have distinct real zeros, confirming Zhang’s conjecture. We also locate the multiple boundary zeros and the two nonreal zeros occurring between the thresholds.
