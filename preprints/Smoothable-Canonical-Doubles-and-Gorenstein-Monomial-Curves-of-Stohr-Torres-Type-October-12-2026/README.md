# [Smoothable canonical doubles and Gorenstein monomial curves of Stöhr–Torres type](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We construct smoothable Gorenstein monomial curves of Stöhr–Torres type for every genus at least 208, giving infinitely many counterexamples to Stevens’s conjecture. We also prove a general smoothability result for sufficiently high canonical doubles of smoothable integral curve singularities.

## 日本語

種数208以上の各種数について、平滑化可能なStöhr–Torres型Gorenstein単項式曲線を構成し、Stevensの予想への無限個の反例を与えます。平滑化可能な整曲線特異点から作る十分高い標準二重化についても、一般的な平滑化の結果を証明します。
