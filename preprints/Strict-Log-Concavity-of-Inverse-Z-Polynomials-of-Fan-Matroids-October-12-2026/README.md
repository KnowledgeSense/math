# [Strict log-concavity of inverse Z-polynomials of fan matroids](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We prove that inverse Z-polynomials of all fan matroids have positive symmetric coefficients and are strictly log-concave at every internal index. The proof uses a binomial-kernel inequality and log-concave weights.
