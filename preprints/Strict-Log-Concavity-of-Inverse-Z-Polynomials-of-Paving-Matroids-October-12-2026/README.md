# [Strict log-concavity of inverse Z-polynomials of paving matroids](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We prove strict log-concavity of inverse Z-polynomial coefficients for every loopless paving matroid of rank at least two. The proof treats both odd and even ranks through normalized coefficient formulas.
