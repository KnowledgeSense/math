# [Strict sign regularity of the reciprocal Jackson q-gamma kernel](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
13 October 2026

For every 0 < q < 1, we prove strict sign regularity of the reciprocal Jackson q-gamma kernel at every finite order on positive real nodes. A Cauchy–Binet expansion gives the prescribed determinant sign and a positive lower bound.
