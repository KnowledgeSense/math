# [Strong terminal failures of log-concavity in independence polynomials of subcubic trees](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We construct trees of maximum degree three with arbitrarily long runs of log-concavity failures at the last possible positions of the independence sequence. Every failure can have an arbitrarily large ratio, with an explicit bound on the number of vertices.
