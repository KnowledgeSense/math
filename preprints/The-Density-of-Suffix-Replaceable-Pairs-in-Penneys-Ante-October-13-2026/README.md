# [The density of suffix-replaceable pairs in Penney’s ante](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
13 October 2026

We prove that the proportion of suffix-replaceable ordered pairs in Penney’s ante converges to a positive limit, with error O(n²2^(−n/3)). The limit is at least 1/512.
