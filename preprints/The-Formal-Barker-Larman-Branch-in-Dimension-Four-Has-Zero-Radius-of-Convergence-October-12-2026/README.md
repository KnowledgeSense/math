# [The formal Barker–Larman branch in dimension four has zero radius of convergence](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We prove that the specified normalized formal branch for the Barker–Larman problem in dimension four has radius of convergence zero in the supremum norm and the stated differentiable, Sobolev, Fourier and analytic norms. The proof uses algebraic three-cycles, a Frobenius identity and equidistribution of small points.
