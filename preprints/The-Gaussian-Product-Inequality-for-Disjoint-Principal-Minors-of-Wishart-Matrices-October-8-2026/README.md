# [The Gaussian product inequality for disjoint principal minors of Wishart matrices](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
8 October 2026

We prove the Gaussian product inequality for determinant powers of disjoint diagonal blocks of a real Wishart matrix, for every admissible real shape parameter and all nonnegative powers. This settles the conjecture of Genest, Ouimet and Richards.
