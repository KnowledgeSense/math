# [The honest embedding dimension of numerical semigroups of multiplicity five](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
12 October 2026

We determine the honest embedding dimension of every numerical semigroup of multiplicity five. Explicit criteria and parametrizations characterize which of these semigroups occur as value semigroups of space curve germs.
