# [Tuza’s conjecture holds for co-chain graphs](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
8 October 2026

We prove that the minimum triangle edge cover is at most twice the maximum edge-disjoint triangle packing in every co-chain graph. Analytic bounds handle large graphs, and explicit packing certificates settle the remaining finite cases.
