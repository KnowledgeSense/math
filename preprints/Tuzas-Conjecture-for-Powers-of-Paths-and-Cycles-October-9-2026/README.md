# [Tuza’s conjecture for powers of paths and cycles](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
9 October 2026

We prove Tuza’s triangle packing and covering inequality for every power of a path or cycle and every finite disjoint union of such graphs. The proof combines explicit constructions from Skolem sequences with the co-chain graph case and finite certificates.
