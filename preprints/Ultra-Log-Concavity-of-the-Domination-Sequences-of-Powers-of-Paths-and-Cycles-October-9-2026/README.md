# [Ultra log-concavity of the domination sequences of powers of paths and cycles](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
9 October 2026

We prove that the domination sequences of all powers of paths and cycles are ultra log-concave, confirming a conjecture of Galvin and Zhang. The proof uses bounded gaps and also establishes log-concavity for a family of restricted compositions.
