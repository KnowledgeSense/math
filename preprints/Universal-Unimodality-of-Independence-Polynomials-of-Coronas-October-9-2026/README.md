# [Universal unimodality of independence polynomials of coronas](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
9 October 2026

We classify the graphs H for which the independence polynomial of G ∘ H is unimodal for every graph G: precisely complete graphs with one edge removed. For every other H, explicit connected cographs provide counterexamples.
