# [Zeros of the independence polynomials of uniform linear hypercombs](article.pdf)

Knowledge Sense
9 October 2026

We locate the zeros of the strong independence polynomials of uniform linear hypercombs outside the sector |arg z| ≤ 2π/3. Their coefficients are consequently strictly log-concave for every spine length and every uniformity at least three.
